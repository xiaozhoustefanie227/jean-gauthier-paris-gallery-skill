# Jean Gauthier × Paris Gallery Skill V4

Version: 4.0.0

A visual direction skill for transforming images into poetic paper-based gallery artworks.

---

# Purpose

This skill creates artworks inspired by:

- private artist notebooks
- Paris gallery archives
- atelier fragments
- unfinished paper studies
- poetic visual memories

The goal is not to create a poster.

The goal is to create the feeling of discovering an artist's private study sheet.

---

# Core Philosophy

Do not redraw the entire image.

Preserve only the emotional fragment.

The artwork should feel:

- collected
- handmade
- imperfect
- quiet
- intimate

---

# Visual Language

## Material

Use:

- aged paper texture
- handmade paper surface
- subtle grain
- collage fragments
- pencil marks
- watercolor traces
- archival labels


Avoid:

- glossy digital surfaces
- modern UI aesthetics
- commercial poster feeling

---

# Composition Rules

## Layout

Preferred:

- asymmetric composition
- large negative space
- small emotional focal point
- gallery archive feeling


Avoid:

- centered poster layout
- symmetrical design
- excessive decoration

---

# Line & Painting Style

Use:

- delicate handwritten lines
- incomplete sketches
- transparent layers
- accidental marks


Avoid:

- clean vector outlines
- cartoon rendering
- perfect illustration

---

# Color Direction

Palette:

- warm ivory
- faded beige
- muted grey
- charcoal black
- faded red
- natural pigments


Avoid:

- neon colors
- high saturation
- synthetic gradients

---

# Image Transformation Workflow

## Step 1 — Observe

Identify:

- strongest emotional element
- meaningful silhouette
- important texture
- memory-like detail


## Step 2 — Reduce

Remove:

- unnecessary information
- realistic background complexity
- excessive details


## Step 3 — Reconstruct

Transform into:

- paper artwork
- artist notebook fragment
- gallery archive piece


## Step 4 — Add Human Imperfection

Include:

- unfinished edges
- handwritten notes
- collage fragments
- subtle mistakes

---

# Supported Subjects

Optimized for:

- portraits
- landscapes
- still life
- furniture
- objects
- poetic visual studies

---

# Negative Prompt

Do NOT create:

- advertising poster
- fashion magazine cover
- clean graphic design
- digital illustration
- 3D render
- cartoon style
- overly polished artwork

---

# Output Feeling

The final image should feel like:

"A forgotten artwork discovered inside a Paris artist archive."
